This Is William’s Speech to Rebecca


the way i see it
依我看来，
if something makes you sad when it ends, it must have been pretty wonderful when it was happening.
如果某件事结束时让你感到悲伤，那么它发生的时候一定非常美好。
truth be told, i’ve always felt it a bit lazy to just think of the world as sad because so much of it is, because everything ends. everything dies.
说实话，我一直觉得把世界简单地看作悲伤有点懒惰，因为很多时候悲伤是因为万物终将消逝。
but if you step back and look at the whole picture,
但如果你退后一步，退后一步，看看全貌。
if you’re brave enough to allow yourself the gift of a really wide perspective,
如果你足够勇敢，让自己拥有一个真正广阔的视角，
and so for his pioneering role in the development of drugs targeting alzheimer’s disease, we’re proud to honor dr marcus brooks.
那么，为了表彰马库斯·布鲁克斯博士在研发阿尔茨海默病药物方面的开创性贡献，我们很荣幸向他致敬。
you know, this all started from a number of cancer drugs that didn’t work.
你知道，这一切都始于一些无效的抗癌药物。
if you do that, you’ll see that the end is not sad, rebecca.
如果你也这样想，你就会明白，结束并不悲伤，丽贝卡，
it’s just the start of the next incredibly beautiful thing.
它只是下一个无比美好事物的开始。
